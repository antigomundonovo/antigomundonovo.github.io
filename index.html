URDIA-HIL

· History Intelligence Layer

History content with evidence behind every claim.

URDIA-HIL is the editorial intelligence workspace of the Antigo Mundo Novo history project. It helps its operator research the past, verify facts against registered sources, compose social content and publish it to their own accounts — with a human approving every single publication.

Research & verification

Historical claims are checked against registered sources with provenance. A deterministic judge classifies what is supported — and what stays unknown.

Editorial opportunities

The system surfaces timely story opportunities (like the real origin of Rio's 1912 bondinho) with why-now context, always reviewed by a human.

Social publishing

Approved content is published to the operator's own TikTok and Instagram accounts through the official platform APIs, with full audit trails.

Audience intelligence

Comments, fact challenges and audience demands are organized so the creator can answer what the public actually asks for.

Our rule: nothing is ever published automatically. Every post is reviewed and approved by a human inside the application, and uncertain facts are labelled as uncertain — never presented as settled history.
